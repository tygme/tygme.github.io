Seal I at working sizes

A medal has to hold up from the website opening down to a browser tab. The emblem stays clear at every size; the lettering is meant to be read at 72 pixels and up.

What goes on it, and why

  • Rim, four virtues: Pioneer, Inventor, Founder, Advisor, each backed by the record: AI in production since 1987, the patent, AI WARE, the advisory practice.
  • Inner ring: Tanvir Y. Goraya, Ph.D., in carved Roman capitals.
  • Motto: Mens et Machina, "mind and machine": modeling human judgment and putting it into machines.
  • Final art: once a design is chosen, it can also be rendered as a photographic bronze medallion.
