On the record

Documents and sources

The claims on this site can be checked. These are the primary documents and public records behind them.

  • SBIR Success Stories at NASA Glenn Research CenterNASA/TM-1999-209649 · December 1999 · Structures section

    NASA's own account of the AI design-of-experiments method from AI WARE's 1990 Phase II contract, its use at NASA and Wright-Patterson, and its commercial customers.

  • "Air Force system offers design advice"Design News · November 19, 1990 · copy on request

    Independent report on the Air Force's Rapid Design System and its episodal associative memory, the program the Rapid Foundry Tooling System grew from.

  • Rapid Foundry Tooling SystemU.S. Air Force Wright Laboratory Success Story · PDF

    The Air Force's own account: pattern design for cast parts cut from two weeks to under two days.

  • "Sand Dollars"Aviation Week & Space Technology · July 3, 1995

    Independent report on the system's 80% reduction in casting-pattern design time at the San Antonio Air Logistics Center.

  • U.S. Patent 8,326,792 B2Adaptive Dynamic Personal Modeling System and Method · sole inventor · 2012

    The MindModeler patent: turning an expert's qualitative judgment into a working quantitative model.

  • U.S. Patent Application 2008/0184154Mathematical Simulation of a Cause Model · with R.J. Boland, Jr. · 2008

    The cause-model simulation behind Theory Garden's software.

  • Advances in Machine Learning (2013)Chapter 1, Artificial Intelligence · pp. 220–229 · reprinted from news coverage, December 2012

    The MindModeler patent featured at length: its case for capturing tacit expertise, and its critique of rule-based systems, OLAP, Bayesian networks and statistics.

  • "IT for Creativity in Problem Formulation"International Conference on Information Systems (ICIS) · 2009 · with Boland, Berente and Hansen

    Theory Garden as a tool for distributed, creative problem formulation by managers and software teams.

  • "Metal-Casting Process Design"JOM-e 49(4), The Minerals, Metals & Materials Society · 1997

    Clustering and associative memory for casting design, with Wright Laboratory's Materials Directorate.

  • "A Computer-Based Adaptive Associative Memory"IEEE International Conference on Systems Engineering · 1991

    With Yoh-Han Pao, Ph.D., K. Komeyli and Maj. Steven LeClair, Ph.D., of the Air Force Materials Directorate.

  • Computer Associates acquires AI WARE01net (Italian) · December 22, 1997

    Press report of the acquisition of AI WARE and its CAD/Chem and Process Advisor products.

  • Computer Associates launches Neugents IIITWeb · August 3, 2000

    CA’s own boilerplate: “the world’s leading business software company.” A 1998 CA release called it “the world leader in mission-critical business software.” Independent rankings differ by year; CA was the first software firm after Microsoft to pass $1 billion in sales, in the late 1980s.

  • CA Neugents launch and coverageComputer Associates press releases, December 1998 · IndustryWeek · Computerworld · InformationWeek · PC Week · archived copies on request

    CA's launch of Neugents, its Technology of the Year honor and client statements, with coverage in seven languages.

  • Computer Associates Form 10-K, fiscal 1999U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission

    CA's revenue and the share of it from Unicenter TNG, the product Neugents were built into.

  • "Optimizing Product Formulations Using Neural Networks"Scientific Computing & Automation (Elsevier) · cover story, September 1992

    Glidden (ICI) and Lord Corporation on CAD/Chem. A copy is available on request.
